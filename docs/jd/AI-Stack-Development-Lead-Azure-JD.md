## About the Role

The AI Stack Development Lead will design, build, and lead the development of the enterprise AI application stack on Microsoft Azure, turning approved AI use cases into reliable, secure, production-grade AI services and agents. The role spans solution architecture, hands-on development, productionisation, and technical leadership of a small AI engineering team.

This is a hands-on lead role. It owns the engineering standards, reusable patterns, and delivery of AI solutions built on Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry, Azure AI Services, and MCP-based agent integrations, working closely with AI Platforms, Cloud Platforms, Data Platforms, Architecture, Security, and business teams.

It ensures AI solutions and agents are well-engineered, governed, observable, cost-efficient, and ready for enterprise adoption, covering corporate AI use cases, centralised productionisation, and function-specific agent building.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead the design and development of AI services, AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) solutions, and copilots on Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry, and Azure AI Services.
- Own the AI application stack architecture, including orchestration frameworks, MCP gateways and tool integrations, APIs, connectors, and the retrieval layer on Azure AI Search.
- Set and enforce engineering standards, reusable patterns, code quality, and design reviews across AI developers.
- Lead and mentor a small team of AI developers; plan sprints, estimate work, and manage technical risk and delivery quality.
- Build and support AI agents that connect securely to approved enterprise data sources, tools, APIs, and systems.
- Drive centralised productionisation of AI use cases, including evaluation, testing, release readiness, monitoring, documentation, and operational handover.
- Establish LLMOps practices with the AI Platforms team: prompt and model versioning, automated evaluation, regression testing, CI/CD, and infrastructure as code.
- Implement responsible AI, security, and governance controls by design, including content safety, prompt-injection defences, PII handling, identity and access, and auditability.
- Work with business teams to assess, prioritise, and onboard AI use cases, and translate them into technical designs and delivery plans.
- Evaluate models, frameworks, and tools; run proofs of concept and provide roadmap inputs.
- Monitor solution quality, usage, cost, and performance, and resolve application, integration, and data-access issues.
- Maintain technical documentation, reusable implementation patterns, and enablement material for AI developers.

- Proven experience leading a small development team or acting as technical lead on AI/ML delivery.
