## About the team

KPMG’s Digital Lighthouse is our Centre of Excellence for Data, AI and emerging technologies. Within it, the AI Engineering team builds the Azure-native AI stack that powers our client solutions and internal assets: reusable platforms, accelerators, reference architectures and the LLMOps tooling that turns promising prototypes into governed, production-grade systems.

We work hand in hand with KPMG’s global alliance with Microsoft, and we anchor everything we build in KPMG’s Trusted AI framework, so that our clients can adopt AI with confidence: responsibly, securely and at scale.

## Role overview

We are looking for an AI Stack Development Lead to own the engineering of our AI stack on Microsoft Azure. You will set the technical direction for how we build Generative AI, agentic and machine-learning solutions on Microsoft Foundry (Azure AI Foundry), Azure OpenAI and the wider Azure data and application platform; lead a squad of engineers through delivery; and stay personally hands-on in the design and code of the most critical components.

The role sits at the intersection of engineering leadership, solution architecture and client delivery. You will industrialise what works, retire what does not, and make sure that everything we ship is secure, observable, cost-efficient and aligned to KPMG’s Trusted AI principles. You will work closely with solution architects, data scientists, cyber and risk specialists, engagement managers, client stakeholders and our Microsoft alliance team.

## Roles and responsibilities

### Technical leadership and architecture

- Own the end-to-end architecture of the AI stack on Azure across the application, orchestration, retrieval, model, data, platform and governance layers, together with its evolution roadmap.
- Define engineering standards, reference architectures, design patterns and reusable accelerators for Generative AI, agentic and ML workloads; enforce them through design authority, design reviews and code reviews.
- Evaluate and select Azure AI services, open-source frameworks and third-party tooling; run structured proofs of concept and publish clear build, buy or adopt recommendations.

### Solution build and delivery

- Lead the design and hands-on development of production-grade solutions, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multi-agent systems, copilots, document intelligence and classical ML, using Azure OpenAI, Microsoft Foundry, Azure AI Search and Azure Machine Learning.
- Act as technical lead across multiple concurrent client engagements and internal asset builds; own estimation, technical planning, sprint execution, technical risk and the quality of deliverables.
- Ensure solutions meet non-functional requirements for performance, scalability, resilience, latency and cost, in line with the Azure Well-Architected Framework.

### Platform engineering, LLMOps and operations

- Establish and run the MLOps/LLMOps toolchain: CI/CD on Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions, infrastructure as code with Bicep or Terraform, environment promotion, prompt and model versioning, automated evaluation and regression testing.
- Build observability and FinOps into the stack: distributed tracing, token and cost telemetry, quality metrics and alerting through Azure Monitor, Application Insights and OpenTelemetry.
- Drive reliability practices for AI workloads: Azure OpenAI capacity and quota management (provisioned throughput versus pay-as-you-go), semantic caching, rate limiting, model routing and fall-back strategies through the AI gateway capabilities of Azure API Management.

### Trusted AI, security and risk

- Embed KPMG Trusted AI and responsible-AI controls into the stack by design: content safety, prompt-injection defences, groundedness and hallucination checks, PII handling, human-in-the-loop checkpoints and full auditability.
- Implement Azure security baselines (Microsoft Entra ID, managed identities, Key Vault, Private Link and VNet integration, RBAC, encryption and data residency) and support threat modelling, security reviews and client risk assessments, including the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications.
- Ensure adherence to the firm’s risk management, information security, independence and data-protection obligations (including the DPDP Act and GDPR) on every engagement.

### Client and practice contribution

- Lead solution design workshops with client stakeholders; translate business problems into technical designs, estimates and delivery plans; present architecture options and trade-offs to senior and CXO audiences.
- Support pursuits through RFP/RFI responses, solution proposals, demos and effort estimates, and contribute to KPMG’s AI propositions and go-to-market assets alongside the Microsoft alliance team.
- Contribute to practice intellectual property: accelerators, playbooks, coding standards, points of view and thought leadership.

### People and capability

- Lead, coach and grow a squad of 4–8 engineers; set goals, give regular feedback and support performance and career conversations.
- Build engineering capability across the practice through training, communities of practice, certification drives and hiring (interview panels, technical case design).
- Model KPMG’s values and a culture of engineering excellence, inclusion and continuous learning.

- 5–8 years of overall software, data or ML engineering experience, of which at least 3 years designing and building AI/ML solutions and at least 2 years delivering production Generative AI workloads on Microsoft Azure.
- Demonstrated technical leadership of an engineering squad (four or more engineers) through full delivery lifecycles, including design ownership, code review and release management.
- Expert-level Python; working proficiency in at least one of C#/.NET, TypeScript/Node.js or Java.
- Azure AI platform: Azure OpenAI (GPT-4.1/GPT-5 family, o-series reasoning models, embeddings, structured outputs, function calling), Microsoft Foundry (model catalogue, Foundry Agent Service, prompt flow, evaluations), Azure AI Search (hybrid and vector search, semantic ranking, indexers and skillsets), Azure AI Document Intelligence, Azure AI Content Safety and Azure AI Language.

- Data and ML platform: Azure Machine Learning (pipelines, managed online endpoints, model registry, MLflow) with Azure Databricks and/or Microsoft Fabric; solid grounding in ML fundamentals, feature engineering and model evaluation.
- DevOps and infrastructure as code: Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions, Terraform or Bicep, Docker and Helm, environment and secrets management, automated testing.

- Architecture: microservices and event-driven design, REST/gRPC/asynchronous APIs, Azure Well-Architected Framework and Cloud Adoption Framework, landing-zone and network patterns (hub-spoke, Private Link, VNet integration).
- Security and privacy for AI systems: OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications, identity and access management (RBAC, managed identities), data classification, PII detection and redaction, audit logging.
